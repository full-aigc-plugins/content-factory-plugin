# Third-Party Notices

Content Factory vendors external skills only when they are declared in `skills.lock.json` with immutable provenance and license metadata.

The current lock contains no external vendor source yet. CF-005 and later integration tasks will add reviewed sources here together with their SPDX identifier, upstream repository, immutable tag/commit, and license evidence.

Plugin-local `content-harness` is not third-party content and is protected by `plugin-local-skills.json`.
