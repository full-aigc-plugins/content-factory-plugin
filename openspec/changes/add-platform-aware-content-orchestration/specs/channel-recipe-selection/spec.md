# Channel recipe selection

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: CRS-001 Channel recipes SHALL precede channel-specific authoring
The harness SHALL select a versioned profile and recipe before channel-specific research, structure, writing, editing or asset planning. Late-stage formatting alone SHALL NOT constitute channel adaptation.

#### Scenario: Long article and short-video script
- **WHEN** the same evidence is used for WeChat long-form and a Douyin script
- **THEN** one recipe produces explanatory prose and the other spoken beats/subtitle drafts, each with its own QA

### Requirement: CRS-002 Recipes SHALL describe stage contracts
Each recipe SHALL identify inputs, outputs, capability bindings, kernel gates, retries, consent and checkpoints. Task-specific routes SHALL omit irrelevant stages.

#### Scenario: Formatting-only task
- **WHEN** the user selects an approved variant and asks only for formatting
- **THEN** the route preserves substantive text and does not force research, rewriting, detection or delivery beyond the requested task

### Requirement: CRS-003 Platform constraints SHALL carry evidence and scope
External limits and rules SHALL identify source, checked date and applicable format/account/region. Editorial preferences SHALL NOT be represented as verified ranking algorithms.

#### Scenario: Unknown publishing limit
- **WHEN** a mandatory limit for the selected account/format is unknown or expired
- **THEN** remote submission is blocked while an explicitly labeled working export remains available

#### Scenario: Unsupported algorithm formula
- **WHEN** a vendor supplies a claim such as a fixed engagement multiplier without evidence
- **THEN** it is not installed as a factual platform rule or a promised performance result

### Requirement: CRS-004 Profiles SHALL NOT create additional local skills
All channel strategies SHALL be profile/recipe/reference data under the single plugin-local content-harness. Vendor skill integrity SHALL remain intact.

#### Scenario: Sixteen channel profiles are added
- **WHEN** the plugin is assembled
- **THEN** channel profiles do not create sixteen self-owned SKILL.md directories and the local skill manifest still contains only content-harness

### Requirement: CRS-005 Original and transformative work SHALL preserve provenance
Structure analysis MAY inform new writing, but transformation SHALL preserve attribution and material-use boundaries rather than disguise copied expression or invent first-person experience.

#### Scenario: Viral post is supplied as inspiration
- **WHEN** the user requests an original channel-native post based on its structure
- **THEN** unsupported personal anecdotes and distinctive source expression are not copied into the new author's voice
