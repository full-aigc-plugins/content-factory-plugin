# Controlled editing

Content Factory uses external humanizer/copy-editing methods as bounded editorial tools.

- Default intensity is `standard`; `light` minimizes structural change and `deep` requires an explicit fact map.
- Automatic editing is limited to two rounds. If the result is still unsatisfactory, return to human review.
- Editing improves clarity, voice, structure and readability. It is never a detector-evasion loop.
- Do not add first-person experience, customer stories, outcomes, quotations or testimonials that are not supported by the source bundle.
- Do not introduce zero-width characters, homoglyph substitutions, deliberate typos or similar obfuscation.
- Protected terms and deterministic fact findings are validated after every vendor candidate.
- Conversation history is not factual source material. Only explicit SourceBundle/ClaimRegistry evidence and user assertions may support content facts.
